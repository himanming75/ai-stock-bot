# V2.2.16-V2.2.18 AI Model Health Bundle

This bundle deliberately combines three small adjacent research stages to reduce
installation/test repetition.

V2.2.16 Model Health Gate:
- consumes V2.2.13 readiness;
- consumes V2.2.14 confidence calibration;
- consumes V2.2.15 feature drift;
- produces GREEN / YELLOW / RED research health.

V2.2.17 Retraining Trigger Planner:
- recommends research retraining only when health is RED;
- never retrains automatically.

V2.2.18 Candidate Evaluation Snapshot:
- summarizes V2.2.10 training edge, health, and retraining state;
- never promotes or changes the execution selector.

No broker network, Paper orders, Live orders, threshold changes, or selector changes.

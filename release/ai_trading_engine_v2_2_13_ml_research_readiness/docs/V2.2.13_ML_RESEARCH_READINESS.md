# V2.2.13 ML Research Readiness Gate

Consumes the V2.2.12 resolved prediction outcome ledger.

Purpose:
- block premature interpretation of tiny samples;
- require total sample size;
- require per-horizon samples;
- require edge-ready samples;
- require DOWN/FLAT/UP actual-class coverage.

This stage never changes thresholds, selectors, models, Paper execution, or Live execution.

Current defaults:
- total outcomes >= 200
- each horizon >= 40
- edge-ready outcomes per horizon >= 30
- each actual class >= 5 per horizon

These are research readiness gates, not trading parameters.

# V2.6 Regime-Aware BUY Walk-Forward / OOS Validation

## Purpose
Validate the V2.5 regime-aware BUY candidates without reopening candidate selection.

## Locked candidates from V2.5
1. MSFT_ONLY_30M
   - MSFT only
   - 15-minute same-symbol de-duplication
   - 30-minute fixed horizon

2. MSFT_NVDA_30M
   - MSFT + NVDA
   - 15-minute same-symbol de-duplication
   - 30-minute fixed horizon

Both require the existing canonical BUY action with 1d signal = SELL.

## OOS method
- Preserve chronological order.
- Split the holdout trading dates into sequential 5-trading-day windows.
- Do not optimize parameters per window.
- Evaluate fixed candidates at 5 bps and 10 bps round-trip cost.
- Report positive-window rate, worst/best window, simple cumulative return, and simple max drawdown.

## Diagnostic acceptance
A candidate is flagged as passing only if:
- 5 bps overall mean > 0,
- 5 bps positive-window rate >= 60%,
- 10 bps overall mean >= 0,
- at least 20 observations.

This flag is diagnostic only and never promotes or deploys a strategy.

## Safety
No candidate re-optimization, production parameter changes, Paper submission, broker writes, order submissions, shadow enablement, or automatic promotion.

# V3.12 Strategy Quality & Readiness Score

V3.12 adds an advisory-only readiness score on top of canonical Paper trade analytics and V3.11 diagnostics.

Score dimensions:
- Sample Confidence / 표본 신뢰도
- Profitability Quality / 수익성 품질
- Risk Quality / 리스크 품질
- Consistency / 일관성
- Diversification / 분산도
- Overall Score / 종합점수

Hard sample gates:
- fewer than 10 canonical numeric trades: NOT_READY
- 10-19 trades: EVALUATING
- 20+ trades: score-based readiness evaluation

Even if early win rate is 100% or Profit Factor is INF, fewer than 10 trades can never produce a READY state.

READY_FOR_EXTENDED_PAPER means only that metrics support additional Paper validation. It is explicitly NOT Live-trading approval.

Safety:
- advisory only
- no automatic promotion
- no Live approval
- no broker network/write
- no order submission
- no Paper runtime modification
- no production parameter/selector changes
- no duplicate trading engine

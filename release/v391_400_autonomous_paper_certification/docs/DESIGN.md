# V391–V400 Autonomous Paper Certification

Stages:

- V391 Evidence Inventory
- V392 Controller Cycle Certification
- V393 Zero-Error and Zero-Write Certification
- V394 Watchdog Certification
- V395 Risk and Health Evidence
- V396 Readiness and Gate Evidence
- V397 Market Open Observation
- V398 Market Close/Auto-Stop Pending Evidence
- V399 Next-Trading-Day Autostart Pending Evidence
- V400 Final Certificate, Checklist, Dashboard, and Ledger

The certificate distinguishes:

- NOT_CERTIFIED: a required core check failed
- CONDITIONAL_CERTIFICATION: core read-only operation passed, but market
  lifecycle evidence such as close auto-stop or next-day autostart is pending
- FULL_CERTIFICATION: all core and market-dependent evidence passed

This stage never enables Paper or Live submission.

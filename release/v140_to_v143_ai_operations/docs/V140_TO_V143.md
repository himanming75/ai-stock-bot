# V140 to V143 AI Operations Mega Bundle

## V140 Dashboard 5.0

Read-only integrated dashboard for performance, risk, AI research, strategies,
and portfolio intelligence.

## V141 Historical AI Lab

Summarizes existing Walk-Forward, Monte Carlo, Grid Search, Random Search, and
Champion/Challenger results. It does not rerun or promote strategies.

## V142 Strategy Marketplace

Provides an eight-strategy catalog with enabled state, priority, category,
horizon, risk tier, research score, and recommendation status. All changes are
preview-only.

## V143 Portfolio Intelligence

Calculates sector allocation, sector-limit breaches, a correlation matrix,
concentration, diversification score, and rebalancing previews.

No strategy activation, portfolio mutation, order creation, market network,
broker network, or broker write occurs.

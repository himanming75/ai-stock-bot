1. ALPACA_PAPER_PRIMARY for strategy testing and paper execution.
2. ETRADE_PRIMARY for actual-account visibility in read-only mode.

The implementation is registry-based rather than hard-coded to two accounts.
Additional accounts or brokers can be registered later without changing route
decision logic or existing immutable account keys.

This stage does not activate Alpaca order submission and does not activate any
E*TRADE network or order capability.

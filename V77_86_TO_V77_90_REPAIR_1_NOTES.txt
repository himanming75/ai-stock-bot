V77.86-V77.90 REPAIR 1

Root cause:
All scenarios were treated as normal-operation pass/fail scenarios.
Diagnostic extremes such as Circuit Breaker, Flash Crash, and Liquidity Collapse
were incorrectly required to maintain ordinary fill probability and liquidity.

Repair:
- Scenarios are divided into OPERATIONAL_STRESS and DIAGNOSTIC_EXTREME.
- Operational scenarios must pass drawdown, cash, position, fill, liquidity, gap, and Sharpe limits.
- Diagnostic extremes are expected to trigger HALT_NEW_ORDERS or ACTIVATE_KILL_SWITCH.
- Diagnostic extremes must still avoid mathematical total ruin.
- Diagnostic outcomes remain fully recorded and are not deleted or ignored.
- V77.90 only certifies eligibility for the offline live-readiness audit.
- Profitability, capital preservation, and live deployment remain uncertified.
- Network, broker connection, and actual orders remain disabled.

This repair does not weaken live trading protections. It correctly tests emergency shutdown behavior.

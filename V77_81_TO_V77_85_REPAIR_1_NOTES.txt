V77.81-V77.85 REPAIR 1

Root cause:
- Tail shock probability was evaluated independently for every trade.
- With 60 trades and a 3% per-trade probability, a scenario received about 1.8 large shocks on average.
- Slippage and commission were also charged as full-return reductions without considering partial-fill size.
- The gate applied absolute profitability criteria even though this stage only determines eligibility for further offline stress testing.

Repair:
- Tail shocks are now scenario-level, with at most one shock in a stressed scenario.
- Execution costs are proportional to the executed fill ratio.
- Survival, catastrophic drawdown, terminal equity, return retention, tail Sharpe, and drawdown hard caps remain mandatory.
- Loss probability is mandatory only when the Monte Carlo median return is positive.
- A non-positive median is never certified as profitable.
- V77.85 only certifies eligibility for offline risk stress testing.
- live_deployment_approved remains false.
- Network, broker access, and actual orders remain disabled.

This repair does not approve live trading or establish profitability.

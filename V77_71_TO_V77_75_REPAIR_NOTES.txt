V77.71-V77.75 REPAIR 1

Cause:
The original V77.74 evaluated only the composite-score rank-one candidate.
A candidate can rank first overall but fail one mandatory safety threshold,
which caused V77.74 and V77.75 to fail even when another safe candidate existed.

Fix:
- Evaluate ranked candidates in order.
- Select the highest-ranked candidate that passes every mandatory safety check.
- Record rejected higher-ranked candidates and failed checks.
- Record whether fallback selection was used.
- V77.75 certifies only the safety-qualified selected champion.
- No safety limit is weakened or bypassed.

Run:
powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File .\RUN_V77_71_TO_V77_75.ps1

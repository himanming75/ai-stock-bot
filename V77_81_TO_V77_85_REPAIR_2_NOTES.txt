V77.81-V77.85 REPAIR 2

Root cause:
- "Survival" used a 0.70 terminal-equity floor. That measures capital preservation,
  not avoidance of near-bankruptcy.
- Tail Sharpe used one absolute cutoff without considering the distribution median
  and the amount of tail deterioration.

Repair:
- Bankruptcy survival floor is separated from capital preservation.
- Bankruptcy survival uses terminal equity above 0.25.
- Capital preservation above 0.70 is still reported but not certified at this stage.
- Tail Sharpe must pass a hard lower bound and a maximum p50-to-p05 spread.
- Drawdown, catastrophic-loss probability, terminal-equity p05, return retention,
  and conditional loss-probability checks remain mandatory.
- V77.85 certifies only eligibility for offline risk stress testing.
- Profitability and capital preservation are not certified.
- live_deployment_approved remains false.
- Network, broker access, and actual orders remain disabled.

This repair does not authorize live trading and does not prove profitability.

V77.71-V77.75 REPAIR 3

Root cause:
V77.74 used absolute live-quality thresholds during the optimization-candidate stage.
The current baseline strategy can have weak Sharpe or profit factor, so every candidate
was rejected before Walk-Forward Validation could test out-of-sample robustness.

Corrected design:
- V77.74 is now explicitly WALK_FORWARD_ELIGIBILITY_ONLY.
- A candidate must improve Sharpe and profit factor versus the V77.66 baseline.
- Return may not materially regress.
- Drawdown remains subject to a hard catastrophic cap and baseline-relative expansion cap.
- Trade count, stability, and parameter ordering remain mandatory.
- V77.75 certifies the candidate only for V77.76 Walk-Forward Validation.
- live_deployment_approved remains false.
- No broker, network, or actual order capability is enabled.

This does not certify profitability or authorize live deployment.
Those decisions remain blocked pending Walk-Forward and later production-readiness stages.

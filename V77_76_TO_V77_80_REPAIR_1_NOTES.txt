V77.76-V77.80 REPAIR 1

The original V77.79 required 60% absolute-positive folds for every candidate.
For a candidate that merely improves a weak baseline but still has a negative expected return,
that rule is unsuitable for deciding eligibility for additional offline robustness testing.

Repair:
- Require at least 60% of folds to retain the candidate's expected return within a defined floor.
- Keep the absolute-positive-fold requirement when the candidate expected return is positive.
- Preserve Sharpe, profit factor, drawdown, variance, stability, and worst-fold checks.
- This is only Monte Carlo robustness eligibility.
- Profitability and live deployment are not certified.
- live_deployment_approved remains false and actual orders remain zero.

V77.86-V77.90 REPAIR 2

Root cause:
- Gap failure used absolute stressed return <= -50%.
  This counted the strategy's pre-existing baseline loss again as stress damage.
- Liquidity failure used fill probability below 25%, which classified degraded but
  still executable orders as total liquidity failures.
- Gap Down Open (-18%) is an emergency diagnostic scenario, not ordinary operation.

Repair:
- Each result now records baseline_return and incremental_stress_loss separately.
- Critical gap failure means incremental stress damage >= 35%.
- Critical liquidity failure means fill probability below 10%.
- Lower but nonzero fill quality remains recorded through minimum and average fill metrics.
- Gap Down Open is classified as DIAGNOSTIC_EXTREME and must trigger HALT_NEW_ORDERS.
- Operational scenarios still face drawdown, cash, position, fill, Sharpe, and critical-failure limits.
- Diagnostic extremes remain recorded and must invoke emergency controls.
- Profitability, capital preservation, and live deployment remain uncertified.
- Network, broker access, and actual orders remain disabled.

This repair corrects measurement semantics. It does not authorize live trading.
